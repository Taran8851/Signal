HOW IT DECIDES

Two ways to decide.
One pipeline.

Use it with no key at all, or plug in your own model for a second opinion on each one. Whichever you use, you can see why something scored what it did — nothing is ranked by company name, and nothing is hidden behind “the algorithm”.

A / WITH YOUR OWN MODEL

“Am I ready for this one?”

The question you actually ask before applying. Plug in your own model — Anthropic, anything OpenAI-compatible, or a server on your own machine — and each opportunity comes back with a straight answer, scored 0 to 10 and always labelled as the model’s words, not ours.

B / NO KEY, NO MODEL

Your words, counted.

Tell Signal the things you care about and the words that put you off. It scores every listing on those, in your browser, for free. You get fewer details than a model gives you, but you can check the arithmetic yourself — and it runs on everything either way.

See the four steps it runs — match, dedupe, threshold, sort →

NEW IN THE DESKTOP APP

Ask it to look.
Ask if it’s open to you.

Two things the app does that a website couldn’t: it reads the web on your machine, and it keeps your CV there too. Both use your own model, and both show you where every answer came from.

RESEARCH

“Find ML summer schools that close in October.”

Ask in plain words. Signal searches, reads the pages and shows you what it found, each with the page it came from. Nothing lands in your inbox until you add it, and it only sets up a search that repeats when you ask it to.

YOUR PROFILE

“Is this one actually open to me?”

Add your CV once. Signal reads the opportunity’s own eligibility rules and answers Open, Likely, Unclear or Not open, quoting the rule it checked. If the page doesn’t say who can apply, the answer is Unclear — never a guess.

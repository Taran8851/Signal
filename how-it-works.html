HOW IT WORKS

No black box.
Four steps and your own words.

Signal is deliberately simple enough to explain in one page. Here is every step between a listing appearing somewhere and it reaching you — and the two things it refuses to do.

WHAT RUNS ON EVERY ITEM

01 / MATCH

Things you care about count more in the title.

A word from your list is worth more where it really means something: 4 points in a title, 3 in the description. Words like “fellowship” or “stipend” add 2 anywhere. Anything on your blocked list goes straight to Archived. That’s the whole scoring rule.

03 / THRESHOLD

How picky you want to be.

Everything is stored; the threshold only decides what is worth interrupting you for. It starts at 3, one solid match. Someone writing to you directly, or a page you watch changing, always gets through.

04 / SORT

Newest first, until a deadline looms.

Recent by default. Switch to deadline when you have a free weekend and want to know what closes soonest, or to match strength when you want the best fits first.

OPEN SOURCES

Add your own source.

Beyond the nine built-in sources, point Signal at a URL you are allowed to read. It keeps the name you give it and is never shown as built-in. Signal does not scrape sites that forbid it.

  • RSS / ATOM FEEDA lab’s news page, a newsletter, a Google Alert you already set up
  • JSON APIYou point at which field is the title, the link, the deadline
  • PAGE WATCHA page with no feed — you hear about it when it changes
  • SOCIAL FEEDAn account you follow, through a feed URL you trust

We don't scrape LinkedIn.

We read the notification mail LinkedIn already sends you, and show where every opportunity came from. Reading that mail is not available in the hosted app yet.

Signal finds you less.

It watches where opportunities appear and shows you the few worth your time.

  • Everything stays here No account, no server. Only the model you pick sees what a task needs.
  • The first check is quiet It saves what it finds first and notifies you about none of it. No backlog.
  • Five short steps Model, you, topics, sources, pickiness. Only one topic is required; change anything later.

Check in the background

Signal keeps checking while it sits in the tray.

The first check starts within a minute of finishing and is quiet: it stores what it finds and notifies you about none of it, so the inbox may look empty at first. After that, new matches arrive as desktop notifications. A message written to you, or a page you watch changing, always reaches you, whatever the bar is set to.
